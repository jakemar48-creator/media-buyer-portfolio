02 / The system behind the lead

Paid traffic is half the job.

The other half is what happens the second a lead comes in. I build that layer in GoHighLevel: routing, tagging, notifications, and follow-up, so nothing sits untouched.

AI-powered lead routing across seven listings. Facebook and LinkedIn lead forms from seven different land properties (Gegeby, Durnamville, Georgia, Tim Floyd, Keese Rd, Odessa, Terlingua) feed into one workflow. A condition step branches by source, tags the contact, and hands off to an AI responder trained on that specific listing.
"1-Acre Property" lead flow. Inbound webhook fires a Meta Conversion API event, creates the contact, tags it, opens an opportunity, and triggers an internal notification and SMS, before anyone touches the lead.
SMS follow-up sequence. A tagged lead gets a message, waits for a reply, then branches: reply removes them from the sequence, silence escalates to the next message across a multi-step cadence.
Conversion API handoff. A "Scheduled Calls" tag fires straight into Meta's Conversion API, so the ad account learns from what happens after the click, not just the click itself.
Booking + reminder flow. Finds the contact, books the appointment, notifies the team, then runs SMS reminders on a wait/branch sequence.
